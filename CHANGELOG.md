# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- a new icon, shown on the marketplace card and on the extension's button

## 1.0.1

- the floating toolbar now looks like Drawdy's own: grouped with dividers, icons on Mirror, Recentre and Stop, and a lime underline only under the active choice
- the guides follow Drawdy's light or dark theme

## 1.0.0

First public release

- turn it on with the button, and every pencil stroke, line and arrow you finish is copied around a centre point
- 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 segments, with Mirror for reflected copies; 6 segments with Mirror on the first time
- dashed guide lines and a centre mark that only you see; Recentre moves them to the middle of your view
- the copies keep the stroke's colour, width, dash and opacity, and the default ink keeps following the board theme
- arrows are copied with Drawdy's standard arrowhead; elbow arrows aren't mirrored, and the bar says so
- all the copies of a stroke are one undo step, so one ⌘Z takes them away and the next takes the stroke
- only what you draw yourself is copied, never a collaborator's drawing, a paste, a duplicate or an undo
- a bar at the bottom centre lights up the Pencil, Line or Arrow you're using in Drawdy's toolbar, holds the segments, Mirror, Recentre and Stop, and says when the tool you picked is one it doesn't mirror
- Stop, Escape or the button again turns it off
- segments and Mirror are remembered between sessions, and it always starts off when a board opens
- a full board or a denied permission gets a short message in the bar
- board and storage permissions are asked when you first turn it on, not when the board loads
