# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- A kaleidoscope button on the board: while it is on, every pencil stroke you finish is copied around a centre point
- 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 segments, with or without mirrored copies
- Dashed guides that only you see mark the centre and the segments; Recentre moves them to the middle of your view
- The copies of one stroke arrive together, so one undo takes them away
- Only your own pencil strokes are copied, never a collaborator's, a paste or an undo
- Stop, Escape or the button again turns it off; your segment and mirror choices are remembered in this browser
