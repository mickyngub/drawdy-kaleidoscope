# Kaleidoscope ✿(◠‿◠)✿

**draw one stroke, get a mandala.**

Every pencil stroke, line and arrow you finish comes back around a centre point, turned and mirrored like a real kaleidoscope. A few squiggles become a snowflake, a flower or a mandala in seconds ✧

![Turning Kaleidoscope on, picking the Pencil in Drawdy's toolbar and drawing two strokes that are copied around the centre in 6 mirrored segments, then a straight line and an arrow with the toolbar's Line and Arrow, then switching to 12 segments and drawing one more stroke.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-kaleidoscope/main/docs/media/kaleidoscope.gif)

---

## let's draw ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **flower** in the extension rail.
2. A bar opens at the bottom of the screen and dashed guide lines fan out from the middle of your view. Pick the **Pencil**, **Line** or **Arrow** in Drawdy's toolbar; the bar lights up the one you're using. Then choose **2**, **3**, **4**, **6**, **8** or **12** segments.
3. Draw. Everything you finish is copied into each segment; collaborators' drawings are left alone.
4. Click **Stop** or press **Escape** when you're done.

> (・_・;) **nothing gets copied?** Only Pencil, Line and Arrow are mirrored; shapes and text are left alone.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Segments**: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 copies around the centre, 6 to start.
- **Mirror**: adds every copy flipped as well, like a real kaleidoscope. It starts on.
- **Recentre**: moves the centre to the middle of your view, so pan first, then click.
- **Guide lines**: dashed spokes and a small ring at the centre, on your screen only. They go when you stop.

---

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- A snowflake: 6 segments with **Mirror**, then short zigzags from the centre outwards.
- A flower: 8 segments, one petal-shaped loop, then a dot in the middle.
- A mandala for a card: start on **12** near the centre, then switch to **6** for the outer petals.
- Pan to an empty spot and click **Recentre** for a second pattern beside the first.

---

**the small print**: nothing downloads, and nothing you draw leaves your board; the copies are ordinary strokes everyone sees, in your colour, width, dash and opacity, and the default ink keeps following the board theme. Your segments and **Mirror** choice stay in this browser. Textured pencil strokes are copied without their texture, arrow copies get Drawdy's standard arrowhead, and elbow arrows aren't mirrored. Drawdy goes back to Select after each line or arrow, so pick **Line** or **Arrow** again for the next one. Bar icons from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC).
