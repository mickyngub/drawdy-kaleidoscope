# Kaleidoscope

Draw like a kaleidoscope. Turn it on, draw one stroke with the pencil and it appears again around a centre point, turned and mirrored, so a few scribbles become a mandala, a snowflake or a flower.

## What it's for

- **Mandalas and rosettes** in a few strokes.
- **Snowflakes, flowers and stars** for a card, a slide or a kid's drawing session.
- **Symmetric doodles** while you think on a call.

## How to use

1. Find the kaleidoscope button with the board's action buttons on the right and press it.
2. A bar appears at the top of the screen and dashed guides show the segments around the middle of your view.
3. Pick the pencil and draw. Each stroke you finish is copied into every segment.
4. In the bar, choose 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 segments, and turn Mirror on or off. Recentre moves the centre to the middle of your view.
5. Press Stop, Escape or the button again to turn it off.

## Tips

- Pan and zoom first, then press Recentre: the centre goes to the middle of what you see.
- One undo takes away the copies of your last stroke; a second undo takes away the stroke itself.
- Six segments with Mirror on gives the classic kaleidoscope look.

## Good to know

- The copies are ordinary pencil strokes. Everyone on the board sees them, and you can move, recolour or delete them like any stroke.
- Only strokes you draw yourself with the pencil are copied. Collaborators' strokes, pasted strokes and undo or redo are left alone.
- The guides are only on your screen and disappear when you turn it off.
- Textured pencil strokes are copied without their texture.
- It always starts off when you open a board, and remembers your segment and mirror choices in this browser.

## Privacy

- Nothing loads from the network and nothing leaves your board.
