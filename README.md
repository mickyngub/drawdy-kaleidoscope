# Kaleidoscope ✿(◠‿◠)✿

**draw one stroke, get a mandala.**

Every pencil stroke, line and arrow you finish comes back around a centre point, turned and mirrored like a real kaleidoscope. A few squiggles become a snowflake, a flower or a mandala in seconds ✧

![Turning Kaleidoscope on, drawing a few pencil strokes that are copied around the centre in 6 mirrored segments, then switching to 12 segments.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-kaleidoscope/main/docs/media/kaleidoscope.gif)

---

## let's draw ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **flower** in the extension rail.
2. A bar opens at the bottom of the screen and dashed guide lines fan out from the middle of your view. Pick **Pencil**, **Line** or **Arrow** in the bar, then **2**, **3**, **4**, **6**, **8** or **12** segments.
3. Draw. Everything you finish is copied into each segment; collaborators' drawings are left alone.
4. Click **Stop** or press **Escape** when you're done.

> (・_・;) **nothing gets copied?** Only Pencil, Line and Arrow are mirrored; shapes and text are left alone.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Segments**: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 copies around the centre, 6 to start.
- **Mirror**: adds every copy flipped as well, like a real kaleidoscope. It starts on.
- **Recentre**: moves the centre to the middle of your view, so pan first, then click.
- **Guide lines**: dashed spokes and a small ring at the centre, on your screen only. They go when you stop.

---

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- A snowflake: 6 segments with **Mirror**, then short zigzags from the centre outwards.
- A flower: 8 segments, one petal-shaped loop, then a dot in the middle.
- A mandala for a card: start on **12** near the centre, then switch to **6** for the outer petals.
- Pan to an empty spot and click **Recentre** for a second pattern beside the first.

---

**the small print**: nothing downloads, and nothing you draw leaves your board; the copies are ordinary strokes everyone sees, in your colour, width, dash and opacity, and the default ink keeps following the board theme. Your segments and **Mirror** choice stay in this browser. Textured pencil strokes are copied without their texture, arrow copies get Drawdy's standard arrowhead, and elbow arrows are mirrored at 2 or 4 segments only.
